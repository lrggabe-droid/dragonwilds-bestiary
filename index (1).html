V1.0 RC3 Spawn-Layer Stress Test
High-density species render as clusters/habitats at world scale; unique bosses remain individual 👑 markers. These are verified location counts, not fabricated exact coordinates. Exact coordinate placement will only be added when the source exposes reliable coordinates.
V1.0 RC3 Coordinate Integrity Layer
Live tracker spawn counts and individual spawn records are source-backed. Numeric X/Y coordinates are not exposed in the searchable guide payload, so this build refuses to infer coordinates from internal spawn IDs. Tap a source button to open that creature's live interactive tracker.
V1.0 RC3 Live Map Expansion
Counts come from the current 1.0 monster index. Exact X/Y remains intentionally withheld until a coordinate payload can be verified. One-off named encounters remain individual-marker candidates; dense populations default to clusters/habitats.
